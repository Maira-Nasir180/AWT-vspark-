# VSPARK 2025 – Prompt Engineering Competition

🌐 **Live Website:** [Visit VSPARK](https://awt-vspark-oml03amv0-maira20.vercel.app/)
**Live Website:** https://awt-vspark-oml03amv0-maira20.vercel.app/

VSPARK is a web-based platform developed for an AI Video Generation and Prompt Engineering Competition at **COMSATS University Islamabad, Vehari Campus**.

The platform allows participants to register their teams, submit payment receipts, access their participant portal, participate in a timed AI video generation challenge, and submit their final video along with the prompts used.

## ✨ Features

- **Home Page:** Provides information about the competition, rules, and registration process.
- **Team Registration:** Allows participants to register their teams.
- **Payment Verification:** Participants can upload payment receipts for admin verification.
- **Participant Portal:** Provides access to the competition portal.
- **Competition Timer:** Offers a 20-minute countdown.
- **Video Submission:** Participants can submit their generated video through a Google Drive link.
- **Prompt Submission:** Participants submit the prompts used to generate their AI videos.
- **Admin Panel:** Provides an interface for managing competition activities.
- **Competition Results:** Displays competition results and announcements.

## 📌 About the Project

VSPARK is designed to provide a digital platform for organizing an AI Video Generation and Prompt Engineering Competition. It allows participants to showcase their creativity, prompt engineering skills, and visual storytelling abilities.

## 📝 How It Works

1. Register your team.
2. Upload the payment receipt.
3. Wait for admin approval.
4. Access the participant portal.
5. Start the 20-minute competition.
6. Generate your AI video.
7. Submit the video link and prompts.

## 🎬 Supported AI Tools

- Runway
- Kling
- Pika
- Sora
- CapCut
- Google Flow

## 📜 Competition Rules

- Participants must bring their own laptops and AI tool accounts.
- Videos must be generated during the competition.
- Pre-made videos are not accepted.
- Participants must submit their video and prompts.
- Judges' decisions are final.
